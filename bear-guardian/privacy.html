Bear Guardian Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Bear Guardian for iOS

Local progress. Sharing by choice. No account required for offline gameplay.

Playing without an account

Bear Guardian (小熊守護夜) is an iPhone game published by LI CHUJIAN. Core gameplay works offline and requires no account. The current app does not send gameplay records, stars, cosmetic selections or achievement progress to a server operated by the publisher.

Data stored on your device

The app saves your best score and completed-wave record, star balance, cosmetic unlocks and selections, achievement progress and claimed rewards, language choice, and sound/vibration preference on your device. These records let you keep your progress and settings between sessions. There is no developer account, cloud-save service or online leaderboard in the current app.

Local records remain until app data is removed. Deleting the app removes its local records; offloading an app can retain its data. Apple device backups may contain app data according to your settings, and restoring a backup may restore it. We cannot access, restore or delete records stored only on your device.

Sharing cards

Camp and score cards are generated on your device. Only after you tap the share button does the app pass the card image and accompanying text to the iOS share sheet. Depending on the card you choose, it includes your selected bear outfit and camp, best score and completed waves, achievement count, or the current run’s score, wave reached and stars collected. The card does not include your total star-wallet balance, account details or a tracking identifier.

The app does not upload cards to the publisher. If you select a recipient, application or service, that destination receives the content you choose to share and processes it under its own privacy practices. Cancelling the share sheet sends no card to a selected destination.

Saving images and permissions

If you choose Save Image in the system share sheet, iOS may ask for permission to add the selected card to your photo library. This is used only to save that card. The game does not read, browse or upload your existing photos. You can manage photo permissions in iOS Settings. Saving a card through Files is handled by the system destination you choose.

The current game does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location-services, notifications or App Tracking Transparency access.

Advertising, analytics and payments

The current app has no advertising, gameplay-analytics SDK, developer-operated crash-reporting service or in-app purchases. Stars are earned through gameplay and achievement rewards; they are not purchased with money. The publisher does not sell player information or use the app for cross-app advertising tracking.

Apple may process information when providing the App Store, TestFlight, device backups or optional diagnostics under its own settings and privacy policy. These platform services are separate from the game’s local records.

This website and external links

These information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process connection information to operate its service; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. These pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, external fonts or cookies set by us. Opening an external link or your email application is subject to that service’s privacy practices.

Support messages and privacy requests

If you email lichujian812@gmail.com, we receive your email address and the information or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate the issue and handle your request, keeping them only as needed for support or applicable obligations. The app does not automatically attach your game records or device identifiers. Please do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal information.

To ask a privacy question or request access, correction or deletion of support information you have sent us, contact lichujian812@gmail.com. Requests about local game records must be handled on the device. The game does not ask for a player’s age or identity; a parent or guardian may contact us about personal information a child has sent through support.

Policy updates

We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s features or data practices change. This policy describes the current app, including its locally saved progress and optional sharing features.
