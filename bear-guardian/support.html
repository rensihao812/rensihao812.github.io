PLAYER SUPPORT

Bear Guardian Support

How do I play?

Read the field guide on the home screen. Move bricks between walls or drag supply bricks directly onto a wall. Foxes consume one brick; boars consume three. Watch the forecast and reinforce the next target before the animal arrives.

Where are my stars, outfits and achievements?

Tap stars when they appear during a run. Open Wardrobe & camp or Watch journal from the home screen to preview cosmetics, see achievement requirements and claim one-time rewards. Progress stays on this device. Some cosmetics require both an achievement and stars; already-owned items can be re-equipped for free.

Can I play offline and keep my records?

Core gameplay and the included artwork work offline, without an account. Opening these information pages or sending support email needs a connection. Progress is stored locally, without developer cloud sync. Deleting the app may remove progress; restoring an Apple backup may restore it.

How do I share or save a card?

Use the share icon on the home screen for your camp and personal records, or Share this watch after a run. Review the card, then open the system share sheet. Available destinations depend on installed apps. Choose Save Image to save the card, allowing photo additions if iOS asks. Cancelling sharing keeps your game progress.

Why did the game pause?

The game pauses when it leaves the foreground or instructions are opened. Return to the game and tap Keep guarding. Check the sound control and device volume if sound or vibration is unavailable.

Feedback and analytics choices

Open Settings & feedback from the home screen to manage gameplay analytics (on by default), or send feedback using Google Forms. No email address is required, and feedback does not depend on the analytics switch.
