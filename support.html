Real Men: Down 100 Support

How do I play Down 100?

Open Play, tap Start Run and hold Left or Right to move. Step off platforms to descend. Reach floor 100 while keeping your health above zero. Open How to Play in the game toolbar for platform rules.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The game and artwork are bundled in the iPhone app. Core gameplay needs no internet connection or account. Rewarded ads, purchases and purchase restoration require internet.

Where are my records?

Open Records. Completed runs and your best score stay on this device. They are not synced to the browser version or another phone. Leaving an active run pauses it. After a first loss, abandoning the run saves the result once; a revived run is saved at its final loss or victory.

Why did the game pause?

It pauses when the app leaves the foreground or when you open instructions. Tap Resume when you are ready.

Why is there no sound?

Check Sound effects in Settings and your phone's volume/silent mode. The game respects silent mode. Haptics also depend on device support.
