Real Men: Down 100 Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026. Applies to app version 1.2.0.

Publisher: rensihao

Privacy contact: rensihao812@gmail.com

Local game data

Personal best, run count, up to 20 recent results (floor, duration, outcome and date), and sound/haptic preferences are saved on your device. The developer does not receive these records. Apple device backups may include them according to your settings. Deleting the app removes local game data; restoring a backup may restore it. Core gameplay works offline and requires no account.

Optional rewarded ads

Players can voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement for one revive per run. Ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers available under device settings, ad interactions, diagnostics and performance information to provide ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. Non-personalized ads still process information.

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Where required, a consent form is presented before ads can be requested. Ad Privacy Options appears in Settings when Google's consent service requires it. Review Google's privacy policy for Google's processing practices. Choosing not to sell or share advertising data does not remove ads or prevent you from earning a revive. Non-personalized ads still require some processing for delivery, fraud prevention and measurement. Google determines retention for information processed by its services under its privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The app uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine Premium access and support restoration. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple's services process payment and purchase information under Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions and support

The app does not request contacts, photos, camera, microphone, location or notification access. It is not designated as a Kids Category app. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, retaining it only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

To ask questions or request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, email rensihao812@gmail.com. Available rights depend on your location. Local game data can be removed by deleting the app; advertising and Apple purchase data are also subject to the respective providers' controls and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
