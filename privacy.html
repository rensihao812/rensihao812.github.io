Real Men: Down 100 Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026. Applies to Real Men: Down 100 for iOS.

Publisher: LI CHUJIAN

Privacy contact: lichujian812@gmail.com

Local game data

Personal best, run count, up to 20 recent results (floor, duration, outcome and date), achievements, stars, cosmetic unlocks and selections, language choice, and sound, music and haptic preferences are saved on your device. These saved records are not synced to a developer account. If you choose to enable optional gameplay analytics, separate statistics described below are sent to Google Firebase. Apple device backups may include them according to your settings. Deleting the app removes local game data; restoring a backup may restore it. Core gameplay works offline and requires no account.

Optional gameplay analytics

Gameplay analytics is off until you choose Allow Analytics. You can turn Share Gameplay Analytics on or off in Settings without affecting gameplay, purchases or access to rewarded ads. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to measure runs started, floors reached, play duration, deaths, revive clicks, revive results (advertisement or Premium), the floor of revival, and successful Premium purchases. Timestamps and local calendar dates help us understand daily play frequency. App/version information, device information and approximate location may also be processed by Google's SDK.

A random installation identifier groups activity from the same installation; it is not your name, email or an account login. Reinstallation or resetting analytics can change it. This data is pseudonymous, not anonymous. Our Firebase Analytics integration does not collect IDFA, and its ad-storage, ad-user-data and ad-personalization consent settings remain denied. Firebase is linked to our AdMob app so permitted analytics data can also inform aggregate advertising and engagement reporting in AdMob. We do not send names, email addresses, precise location or payment-card details in gameplay events.

Turning analytics off stops future collection, clears pending local gameplay reports and resets the local analytics identity. It does not by itself delete statistics already received by Google. Google processes and retains analytics data under its policies and our Analytics property settings; see Firebase privacy information. An interrupted run may be reported on the next launch using its last saved checkpoint. Offline delivery and analytics reports are not a complete or immediate transaction ledger.

Advertising availability

Rewarded advertisements are disabled in version 1.4.0 build 19. This release does not offer watch-ad revivals or bonus-star advertisements, initialize advertising or consent services, or request advertisements. The Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform SDKs remain bundled for compatibility. The description below applies to earlier test builds in which advertising was enabled.

Rewarded ads in earlier test builds

Players can voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement for one revive per run. Ads use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform. These services may process IP address, device and advertising identifiers available under device settings, approximate location inferred from IP address, ads viewed and ad interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and performance information to provide ads, prevent fraud, and measure performance. Non-personalized ads still process information.

The app requests non-personalized ads and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission. Where required, a consent form is presented before ads can be requested. Ad Privacy Options appears in Settings when Google's consent service requires it. Review Google's privacy policy for Google's processing practices. Choosing not to sell or share advertising data does not remove ads or prevent you from earning a revive. Non-personalized ads still require some processing for delivery, fraud prevention and measurement. Google determines retention for information processed by its services under its privacy policy.

Purchases

Optional Premium is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. The app uses verified StoreKit transaction information to determine Premium access and support restoration. The developer does not receive your card details. Apple's services process payment and purchase information under Apple's privacy policy.

In-app feedback

Settings → Feedback opens a Google Forms page where you can choose a feedback category and enter details. Google hosts and processes the form under its privacy policy. The developer receives your submitted category and text to investigate issues and improve the game. We do not require or collect your email address through this form, but receive any personal information you voluntarily include in your message. Google may process browser and connection information to operate and protect its service. The game does not automatically attach gameplay records, analytics identifiers or app/device versions. Feedback is independent of gameplay analytics consent. We retain support information only as needed to handle your request or meet applicable obligations. See Google’s privacy policy.

Sharing and saving cards

Result and achievement cards are generated on your device. When you tap Share, the app passes only the selected card image to the iOS share sheet. The card can contain your game result or achievement, date, and selected cosmetic appearance. The game does not upload the card to the developer or attach an analytics identifier. If you choose a destination, that app or service receives the image and handles it under its own privacy practices.

If you choose Save Image in the share sheet, iOS may ask for permission to add that image to your photo library. This permission is used only to save the card you selected. The game does not read, browse or upload your existing photos. You can manage photo permissions in iOS Settings.

Permissions and support

The app does not request contacts, camera, microphone, location-services or notification access. Approximate location may still be inferred from network information by the Google services described above. It is not designated as a Kids Category app. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use that information to respond and resolve your request, retaining it only as needed for support or applicable obligations. Please do not send passwords or payment details.

To ask questions or request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us, email lichujian812@gmail.com. Available rights depend on your location. Local game data can be removed by deleting the app; advertising and Apple purchase data are also subject to the respective providers' controls and policies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
